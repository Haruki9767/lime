## Interpretation rules

- Record observations, not guarantees.
- Distinguish no answer, wrong answer, uncited answer, wrong citation, and correct citation.
- Recheck important facts against the canonical source.
- Do not infer causation from one run or one engine.
- Annotate material content, schema, domain, or product changes before comparing runs.
